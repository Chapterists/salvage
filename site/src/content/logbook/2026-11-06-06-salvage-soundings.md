---
title: salvage soundings
date: 2026-11-06
---

`salvage soundings` reads a memreport or LLM CSV from an Unreal Engine game, shows where the memory goes, and measures the run against 8, 12 and 16 GB machines. Give it two captures and it shows what changed. It measures memory; it doesn't save any by itself. There's no release yet; it builds from source.
