---
title: Salvage is Chapterists' toolbox
date: 2026-11-06
---

Salvage is now Chapterists' open-source toolbox: the tools we use to make our stories. Tools can be any shape, from command-line programs to Mac apps and Unreal plugins, and each one is released on its own. The Unreal Engine work carries on as the first two workstreams, Lighter Unreal and Unreal on Mac. The first tool in the box is the `salvage` command.
