---
title: Bringing CAD into Unreal on a Mac
date: 2026-11-06
---

A new chart covers getting STEP and IGES files into Unreal on a Mac, where Epic's Datasmith CAD importer doesn't run. It points to Epic's Mac exporters for SketchUp, Archicad and Rhino, and otherwise to converting with Mayo, an open-source CAD converter. It's honest about what a conversion loses compared with Datasmith, and about what we haven't tried yet.
