title: salvage doctor
date: 2026-11-06
---

The first tool in the repository. `salvage doctor` runs 12 checks on a Mac for the things that make Unreal Engine slow or broken, from Xcode and the Metal Toolchain to free space, caches and swap, and says how to fix each one. On Windows and Linux it runs the 6 checks that aren't Mac-specific. There's no release yet; it builds from source.
