---
title: salvage trim, and the first sounding
date: 2026-11-06
---

`salvage trim` gives an Unreal project memory tiers for 8, 12 and 16 GB machines, using the engine's own device profiles and memory buckets. Every value comes with its reason, and `--undo` takes it all out. It's the first job in the toolbox that saves memory rather than measuring it. On Epic's Third Person template on a 16 GB M4 Mac, the 8 GB tier took the game from 7,687 MB to 6,583 MB with no change in frame rate. The sounding has the method and the spread across runs, and it's plain about what the numbers don't show yet: the smaller tiers ran on a 16 GB machine, there's no packaged build, and the editor preset isn't measured.
